# Assets

Tracked starter packs do not include event logos, attendee lists, sponsor assets, venue photos, badge scans, recordings, or budget sheets.

Private packs may add:

- approved venue shortlists
- room layouts
- speaker headshots and bios with permission
- sponsor logo rules
- slide templates
- signage specs
- budget trackers
- post-event photo selections

Keep private or licensed assets under `.local/content-programs/<profile-id>/event-marketing-operating-system/assets/` or a profile-specific event program.
