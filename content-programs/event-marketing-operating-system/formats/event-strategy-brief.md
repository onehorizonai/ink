# Event Strategy Brief Format

## Use When

- Deciding whether to host, attend, sponsor, speak at, exhibit at, or skip an event.
- Turning an event idea into a clear marketing and operations brief.

## Required Output

1. Event name or working title.
2. Host/attend posture.
3. Audience and reason they would care.
4. Event promise and format.
5. Strategic goal and secondary goals.
6. Budget level, sponsor/partner constraints, and manual risk.
7. Acquisition or target-meeting plan.
8. Follow-up path.
9. KPIs and decision criteria.

## Rules

- Choose posture before writing promotion copy.
- Define the room or audience before defining speakers.
- Include opportunity cost and failure mode, not just upside.
- State what Ink can draft and what remains manual.
