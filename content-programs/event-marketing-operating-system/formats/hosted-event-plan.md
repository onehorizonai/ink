# Hosted Event Plan Format

## Use When

- The brand owns registration, venue/platform, hospitality, programming, and follow-up.

## Required Output

1. Event promise, audience, capacity, and date/location assumptions.
2. Registration and reminder plan.
3. Venue/platform, room layout, AV, WiFi, accessibility, signage, and check-in.
4. Food, beverages, dietary needs, alcohol policy, cleanup, and receipt owner.
5. Speaker, sponsor, host, moderator, and operator roles.
6. Run of show with setup and teardown.
7. Capture, consent, and content reuse plan.
8. Follow-up segments and owners.
9. Budget, reimbursement, and risk notes.

## Rules

- Write logistics as owner-based tasks, not vague checklist wishes.
- Include no-show assumption and minimum viable attendance.
- Prepare follow-up before the event starts.
- Do not hide venue, beverage, AV, accessibility, or receipt details.
