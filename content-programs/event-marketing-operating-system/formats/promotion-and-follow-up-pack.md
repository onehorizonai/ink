# Promotion And Follow-Up Pack Format

## Use When

- Filling a hosted event, promoting event attendance, supporting speakers/partners, or routing post-event follow-up.

## Required Output

1. Registration page copy or event listing copy.
2. Email invite and reminder sequence.
3. Social posts for owned channels.
4. Speaker/partner share kit.
5. Community or local ecosystem post.
6. Live capture prompts.
7. Attendee, no-show, VIP, partner, and speaker follow-up.
8. Recap post or replay copy.
9. Source/UTM and KPI notes.

## Rules

- Segment follow-up by behavior and intent.
- Reminders should add value or logistics, not just urgency.
- Recap content should teach something, not only thank attendees.
