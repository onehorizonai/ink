# Caption Format

## Use When

- A recap card needs channel-native copy for a selected surface, such as Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, X, Threads, LinkedIn, Facebook, or a community post.

## Structure

1. One short hook that frames the card.
2. One to three sentences that connect the joke to an audience pain.
3. Optional restrained hashtags or keywords where native to the channel.

## Avoid

- Repeating all three card bullets in the caption.
- Overexplaining the joke.
- Hashtag spam.
- Pretending the recap is a literal personal diary unless that is the program's stated premise.
