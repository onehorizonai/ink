# Recap Card Format

## Use When

- The program needs a single image with a fixed visual template.
- The idea can fit into three short bullets.
- The target audience will recognize the situation quickly.

## Structure

1. Short title or fixed series label.
2. Optional subtitle or time marker.
3. Three bullets, each one sentence or sentence fragment.

## Copy Rules

- Keep each bullet under 90 characters when possible.
- Make the bullets concrete and visual.
- Use one clear comedic or emotional lane per card.
- Keep the final bullet as the strongest turn when possible.

## Avoid

- Dense explanations.
- Generic productivity claims.
- Jokes that depend on private context.
- More than three bullets unless the private template explicitly supports it.
