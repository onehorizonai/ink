# Newsletter Issue

## Use When

- The output is an email newsletter, digest, or editorial send.
- The audience has subscribed or otherwise expects this editorial or marketing email.

## Structure

1. Subject options.
2. Preview text.
3. Opening.
4. Main story or sections.
5. CTA.
6. Segment, send, and deliverability notes.

## Avoid

- Overloading the issue with too many asks.
- Unsupported claims.
- Treating the newsletter like a social caption.
- Using this format for cold outreach. For outbound cold email, load `.agents/channel-content-writer/references/channels/email.md` and `.agents/channel-content-writer/references/formats/email-cold-outreach.md`.
