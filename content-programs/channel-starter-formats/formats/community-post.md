# Community Post

## Use When

- The output is for Discord, Slack communities, forums, Hacker News, Product Hunt, Quora, or similar community surfaces.

## Structure

1. Native title or opening.
2. Context.
3. Specific question, field note, or useful contribution.
4. Discussion prompt.
5. Rule or moderation risk notes.

## Avoid

- Obvious product pitching.
- Hashtags.
- Cross-post copy that ignores the community's norms.
