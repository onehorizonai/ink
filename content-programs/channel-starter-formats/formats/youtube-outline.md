# YouTube Outline

## Use When

- The output is a long-form YouTube video, educational video, demo, teardown, or commentary.

## Structure

1. Title options.
2. Thumbnail concepts.
3. Opening hook.
4. Section outline.
5. Talking points.
6. CTA.
7. B-roll, screen recording, or asset needs.

## Avoid

- A blog outline pasted into video form.
- Missing thumbnail/title strategy.
- Claims that cannot be shown or sourced.
