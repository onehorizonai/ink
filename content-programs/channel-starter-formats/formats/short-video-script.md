# Short Video Script

## Use When

- The output is for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, or another short vertical video surface.

## Structure

1. Hook.
2. Three to five beats.
3. On-screen text.
4. Shot list or background video brief.
5. Caption.
6. Manual editing or publishing notes.

## Avoid

- Writing only a caption when the channel needs a script.
- Assuming the video has been generated or edited.
- Long setup before the first beat.
