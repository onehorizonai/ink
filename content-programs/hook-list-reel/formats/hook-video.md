# Hook Video Format

## Use When

- The run needs an Instagram Reel video or asset brief that can stop the scroll before the long description carries the depth.

## Structure

1. Topic and target audience.
2. Hook variants, with one selected final overlay hook.
3. First-frame visual idea.
4. Video or footage brief.
5. Overlay text and subtitle notes.
6. Cover or thumbnail guidance.
7. Manual editing and publishing checks.

## Check

- The selected hook is short, specific, and readable on a phone.
- The first frame communicates the topic before the viewer reads the description.
- The asset brief asks for original, watermark-free video that starts with motion or visual change.
- Critical text and subject matter stay clear of Instagram Reels UI overlays.
- The video can be understood without sound.
