# Workflow

## Inputs

- Target audience or selected profile audience.
- One topic, pain, question, workflow, tool, product category, or search-intent cluster.
- Primary keyword and 5 to 12 adjacent terms, if known.
- Available original footage, generated visual concept, or asset constraints.
- Tone boundaries, claim constraints, and any required brand or campaign tag.

## Steps

1. Choose one specific Reel topic that can be understood from the video hook and expanded in the description list.
2. Generate 5 to 10 hook variants, then select one that is short, specific, curiosity-building, and honest.
3. Create the `hook-video` brief with original footage guidance, overlay text, safe-zone notes, cover idea, subtitles, and audio considerations.
4. Draft the `keyword-list-description` with a strong first line, then a long scannable list that naturally includes the primary keyword, adjacent terms, audience phrases, and use-case language.
5. Draft `five-hashtag-set` with exactly 5 relevant hashtags at the bottom of the description.
6. Add a manual QA checklist covering footage rights, audio rights, watermark-free export, mobile preview, safe zones, thumbnail, Trial Reel or A/B options, publishing, scheduling, and early comment follow-up.
7. Add the planned run to `calendar.csv` only when the user wants the schedule persisted.
8. After publishing, add the publish URL and provided metrics to `performance.csv`.

## Guardrails

- Do not claim Ink sourced footage, edited video, chose trending audio, previewed the Reel in-app, published, scheduled, or replied to comments unless a real tool or human did it.
- Do not use recycled watermarked video, low-effort reposts, or visual assets without clear rights.
- Do not use false hooks, generic engagement bait, or hashtags that promise a different topic than the Reel delivers.
- Do not stuff keywords into the list. Search terms should help classification while still reading naturally to a human.
- Do not output more or fewer than 5 hashtags for this program.
- Keep broad Instagram/Reels optimization guidance in `.agents/channel-content-writer/references/instagram-reels.md`; keep this workflow focused on the hook-list format.

## Output Bundle

Each run should include:

- run metadata
- hook variants and selected overlay hook
- video asset brief
- cover or thumbnail guidance
- keyword cluster
- long list-style Reel description
- exactly 5 bottom hashtags
- manual editing and publishing checklist
- review risks and claims to verify
- suggested performance metrics
