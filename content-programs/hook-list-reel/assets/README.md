# Assets

Tracked starter packs do not include video footage, audio, thumbnails, brand templates, account screenshots, or generated media.

Private packs may add:

- approved footage library notes
- video editing template links
- safe-zone overlay references
- cover or thumbnail templates
- subtitle style notes
- approved audio or rights notes
- rendered output folder

Keep private or licensed assets under `.local/content-programs/<profile-id>/hook-list-reel/assets/` unless they are intentionally reusable by the open-source repo.
