# Carousel Concept Format

## Use When

- The post needs several related meme slides around one theme.

## Structure

1. Theme.
2. Target audience.
3. Slide count.
4. Slide arc, such as escalation, roles, symptoms, mistakes, or before/after.

## Check

- The slides belong together.
- The carousel can be understood without a long caption.
- The theme is specific enough to avoid generic workplace jokes.
