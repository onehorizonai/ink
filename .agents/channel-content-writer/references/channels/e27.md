## Native Behavior

### Published rules — concise summary

The [current programme page](https://e27.co/contributor/) specifies:

- Opinion and lessons relevant to Southeast Asian tech/startups; original voice, evidence, plain language, short paragraphs, subheadings.
- Aim for 700–1,000 words; ceiling 1,500.
- No PR, listicles, product/service promotion, or achievement pitches. Company examples must advance the analysis.
- Credible research links are welcome; promotional, personal-site, and SEO backlinks are barred.
- Submit from the author's own account; others must not manage it. Complete photo, bio, location, and social link first.
- Submission portal: [contribute](https://e27.co/contribute/); tracking: [dashboard](https://e27.co/contribute/dashboard/). Editors review submissions; typical publication takes 12–14 working days, without a guarantee.
- Optional credited image: recommended horizontal 690 × 390; editors can supply one.
- Prefer original work. LinkedIn/personal-blog adaptations may be considered with original attribution; prior publication by another news outlet is excluded. After e27 publication, wait one week before republication and credit e27.
- Harmful/illegal/restricted promotion is excluded.
- A linked company profile can carry company visibility separately from the argument.

### Boundaries and unresolved details

No explicit AI-assistance/disclosure policy was located in the reviewed contributor materials. That is not permission or a ban. The author must verify and own the final argument; check the live portal for additional declarations. Login-only fields and account readiness were not inspected. Do not invent field limits or an editor's email from obfuscated text.

The contributor page describes free/unlimited publishing; the [membership page](https://e27.co/membership/?joinpro=1) lists plan-dependent article allowances. Treat fees/quotas as unresolved account details to verify in the portal rather than a reason to buy a plan. Avoid reach promises and named-editor dependencies.

## Audience And Context Rules

e27's [mission](https://e27.co/about/) centres on Southeast Asia's technology ecosystem. Ink's working reader: a founder, CTO, operator, or investor who needs a better business or delivery decision. This reader definition is an editorial choice, not a demographic measurement.

Before drafting:

1. Resolve the Ink profile, workspace, and author through the normal contract and One Horizon tools.
2. Read the author's `Profile`, `Current Work`, and `Work History`; load relevant generated GTM snapshot sections when present. GTM owns current company positioning; author docs own voice and personal history.
3. Find `Ink Channel - e27 - {Author Name}` in that workspace, then fetch its full body. Use its personal boundaries and proposed themes. If absent, use verified author context and flag the missing publication brief; do not borrow another author's.
4. Inspect selected-profile published examples and planned work for overlap when developing a new idea. Use the generic channel workspace for e27 drafts, never the owned-blog publishing folder.
5. Write one sentence explaining the relevance to a specific regional reader. Support regional facts with current primary sources. Do not turn one country's evidence into an ASEAN-wide claim or invent the author's Southeast Asian customers, operations, or experience.

## Compliance And Risk

Apply the published rules above. Check publication history, sources, image rights, commercial relationships, and permission for named examples. Distinguish internal estimates from measured results. Do not append e27's standard editor disclaimer or “Also Read” modules yourself.

## Manual Boundary

Ink prepares the requested pitch, draft, or submission package. The author reviews, completes their profile, pastes the final article into their account, and handles editorial replies. No acceptance, outreach, account creation, or submission is implied by generating a package.

If tracked through One Horizon, use the existing generic-channel hierarchy under `Ink - Channel Content`, channel `e27`, and `In Review` for human action. Do not create an owned-blog publishing PR or mark the article published before a live URL exists.

## Metrics

Record submission date, editorial feedback, acceptance, live URL, and useful follow-up conversations. Do not label the examples above “winning” or “top performing”: no article-level performance data was verified.

## QA Checklist

- Current rules and selected-author brief are loaded; publication history is stated.
- The argument adds something beyond recent e27 coverage and has a credible regional reader payoff.
- The body passes the published-rule summary and the thought-leadership review sequence.
- The private handoff includes word count, sources, unresolved claims, bio, image decision, and account/submission steps.

## Anti-Patterns

An owned-blog SEO rewrite, a LinkedIn post padded into paragraphs, generic AI predictions, fake local anecdotes, thin “human oversight matters” commentary, a One Horizon feature tour, signup links, and copying publisher-added page furniture.
