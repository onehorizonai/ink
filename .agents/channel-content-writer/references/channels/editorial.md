# Contributor Publication Family Guidance

## Source Guidance

Use the destination's current contributor rules and a small sample of actual contributor articles. The initial supported destination is [e27](https://e27.co/contributor/); its specific rules live in `e27.md`.

## Native Behavior

An editor and an independent readership are the audience. Earn attention through a useful argument rather than a commercial offer. Acceptance is an editorial decision.

## Audience And Context Rules

Resolve the selected profile and author context first. Separate professional expertise, verified firsthand experience, external evidence, and hypotheses. Match the publication's readers without inventing local authority.

## Creative Strategy

Choose a concrete tension and a defensible thesis. Explain the mechanism, show a real example, acknowledge a limitation, and end with a decision the reader can use. Define specialist terms when first introduced.

## Compliance And Risk

Check attribution, source dates, publication history, permission to discuss customer work, and commercial interests. Never manufacture anecdotes, quotes, results, or endorsements. Publication rules override generic conversion-copy advice.

## Manual Boundary

Ink prepares pitches, articles, revision responses, and submission notes. The author owns the argument, factual approval, account, and submission. Record submitted, accepted, and published as distinct states.

## Metrics

Track editorial feedback, acceptance, publication URLs, useful reader conversations, and invitations. Do not invent readership or promise distribution.

## QA Checklist

- A clear thesis and useful evidence survive without the author's company name.
- Sources support the exact claims; limitations remain visible.
- Destination rules and author context are loaded.
- Article copy and private handoff notes are separate.

## Anti-Patterns

Disguised advertorials, keyword stuffing, borrowed personal stories, invented regional expertise, copied headlines, and treating submission as publication.
