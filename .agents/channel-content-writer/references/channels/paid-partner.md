# Paid And Partner Channel Family Guidance

Use this family guide for paid ads, partner marketing, affiliates, influencers, sponsorships, and co-marketing handoffs.

## Source Guidance

- Google responsive search ad creative guidance: https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6167122
- Meta ad creative and placement guidance: https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/ad-creative
- TikTok Creative Center and creative best practices: https://ads.us.tiktok.com/help/article/creative-center and https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/creative-best-practices
- Reddit and Pinterest ad creative guidance: https://www.business.reddit.com/copy-creative-best-practices and https://business.pinterest.com/creative-best-practices/
- FTC endorsement guidance for creators, affiliates, partners, sponsorships, and material connections: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides

## Native Behavior

- Paid and partner outputs sit inside an auction, partner relationship, or sponsored context. Creative quality, policy fit, landing-page alignment, disclosure, and measurement matter as much as copy.
- Paid search captures declared intent; paid social creates or redirects attention; partner/influencer content borrows trust from another audience.
- A good paid/partner handoff separates message strategy from execution details: placement, audience, asset, copy fields, landing page, disclosure, approval, and tracking.

## Audience And Context Rules

- Define funnel stage: awareness, consideration, conversion, retargeting, expansion, winback, launch, recruitment, or partner education.
- Name the audience's current belief and the belief the asset must change.
- Clarify offer, proof, landing page, targeting assumptions, placement, and budget sensitivity when known.
- For partner/influencer work, define the partner's audience, voice, rights, disclosure, prohibited claims, and approval process.
- For affiliates, distinguish allowed claims from the affiliate's own editorial perspective.

## Creative Strategy

- Build multiple angles, not small word swaps. Test problem, outcome, proof, objection, category, competitor, urgency, and social-proof angles separately.
- Match creative to placement. Search ads need intent-match and differentiation; social ads need thumb-stop, native visual, and fast value; partner briefs need adaptable talking points.
- Keep copy fields distinct. Do not repeat the same sentence across image text, headline, primary text, and description.
- Tie every ad to a landing-page promise. The click should feel like the next logical step, not a bait-and-switch.
- Include creative fatigue planning: variant families, refresh triggers, and what to learn from winners/losers.

## Compliance And Risk

- Flag platform policy risks before polishing: restricted categories, financial/health/employment claims, personalized attributes, before/after promises, prohibited products, and landing-page mismatch.
- Disclose material connections clearly and conspicuously for creators, affiliates, sponsorships, gifted products, and paid endorsements.
- Do not invent customer proof, "best" rankings, limited-time offers, prices, guarantees, or savings.
- Ensure UTM/tracking, consent, pixel, and privacy requirements are handled by the human/operator.

## Manual Boundary

Ink can draft copy variants, creative briefs, creator briefs, disclosure notes, landing-page dependencies, testing plans, and QA checklists. Ad setup, targeting, bidding, budget, pixel/event setup, partner negotiation, creator posting, affiliate tracking, and reporting remain manual unless explicitly tooled.

## Metrics

- Paid search: impressions, CTR, quality/ad strength, CPC, conversion rate, CPA, search terms, and landing-page conversion.
- Paid social/video: thumb-stop/hook retention, CTR, CPC, CPA, ROAS, conversion quality, frequency, fatigue, and creative-level performance.
- Partner/influencer/affiliate: reach, engagement quality, clicks, code usage, affiliate revenue, partner-sourced pipeline, content reuse, and compliance incidents.

## QA Checklist

- Objective, placement, audience, offer, landing page, and proof are explicit.
- Each variant tests a meaningful angle.
- Disclosure and policy risks are visible.
- Claims are supportable and landing-page aligned.
- Manual setup/tracking/approval tasks are separated.
- Success metrics match the channel and funnel stage.

## Anti-Patterns

- Treating ads as generic social posts with a CTA button.
- Testing ten tiny copy changes instead of distinct angles.
- Hiding disclosures or material connections.
- Sending traffic to a mismatched or weak landing page.
- Overusing urgency, discounts, or guarantees without proof.
- Letting partner content drift into claims the brand cannot approve.
