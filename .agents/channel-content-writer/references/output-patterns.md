# Output Patterns

Use these patterns when the user or program format does not provide a stricter shape.

## Instagram Or Facebook Post

- Caption
- Visual or carousel brief
- Alt text
- Hashtag set, restrained and relevant
- First comment when useful

## TikTok, Reels, Or YouTube Shorts

- Hook
- Beat-by-beat script
- On-screen text
- Shot list or background video brief
- Caption
- Manual editing or publishing steps

For Instagram Reels, also read `instagram-reels.md` and apply its original-content, mobile safe-zone, caption SEO, accessibility, hashtag, and performance guidance.

## YouTube Long Form

- Title options
- Thumbnail concept
- Opening hook
- Section outline
- Talking points
- CTA
- Asset or B-roll needs

## Newsletter Or Email

- Subject options
- Preview text
- Body
- CTA
- Segment and send notes
- Risk notes for deliverability or claims

## Community Or Forum

- Native title or opener
- Body
- Discussion prompt
- Rule/moderation risk notes
- What not to include

## Paid, Partner, Or Influencer

- Objective
- Audience and placement
- Copy variants
- Creative brief
- Claim/compliance notes
- Landing page or tracking dependencies

## Product Or Direct Messaging

- Trigger
- Audience segment
- Message
- CTA
- Frequency and fatigue notes
- Success metric

## Event Or Sales Asset

- Audience and context
- Core message
- Outline or slide spine
- Proof needed
- Follow-up asset ideas
