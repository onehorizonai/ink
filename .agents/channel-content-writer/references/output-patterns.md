# Output Patterns

Use these patterns as a fallback after loading `channel-format-index.md` and the registry row for the requested slug. Prefer the specific format guide from `references/formats/` whenever one applies.

## Instagram Or Facebook Post

- Caption
- Visual or carousel brief
- Alt text
- Hashtag set, restrained and relevant
- First comment when useful

## TikTok, Reels, Or YouTube Shorts

- Hook
- Beat-by-beat script
- On-screen text
- Shot list or background video brief
- Caption
- Manual editing or publishing steps

For Instagram Reels, load the registry row for `instagram-reels`, including the social/video family guides, `channels/instagram.md`, `formats/short-video.md`, and `formats/instagram-reels.md`.

## YouTube Long Form

- Title options
- Thumbnail concept
- Opening hook
- Section outline
- Talking points
- CTA
- Asset or B-roll needs

## Newsletter Or Editorial Email

- Subject options
- Preview text
- Body
- CTA
- Segment and send notes
- Risk notes for deliverability or claims

For cold outreach, do not use this newsletter shape. Load the registry row for `email-sequence`, then apply `formats/email-cold-outreach.md`.

## Community Or Forum

- Native title or opener
- Body
- Discussion prompt
- Rule/moderation risk notes
- What not to include

## Paid, Partner, Or Influencer

- Objective
- Audience and placement
- Copy variants
- Creative brief
- Claim/compliance notes
- Landing page or tracking dependencies

## Product Or Direct Messaging

- Trigger
- Audience segment
- Message
- CTA
- Frequency and fatigue notes
- Success metric

## Event Or Sales Asset

- Audience and context
- Core message
- Outline or slide spine
- Proof needed
- Follow-up asset ideas
