# Social Post Format Playbook

Use this format for single social posts, short updates, native replies, quote-post framing, and lightweight announcement posts across generic social channels. Load the relevant channel or family guide first.

## Source Guidance

- Meta ad and creator guidance: https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/ad-creative and https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/best-practices-education-hub-creators-instagram/
- X creative specs and formats: https://business.x.com/en/help/campaign-setup/creative-ad-specifications and https://business.x.com/en/advertising/formats
- Pinterest creative best practices: https://business.pinterest.com/creative-best-practices/
- FTC endorsement guidance: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides

## Use Cases

- Announce a launch, update, event, resource, hiring need, partnership, milestone, or customer proof.
- Teach one insight, show one artifact, answer one common objection, or start one relevant conversation.
- Repurpose a larger asset into a native post without dumping the whole asset into the feed.

## Required Inputs

- Channel slug, account voice, target audience, and relationship to the audience.
- One job for the post: awareness, save, share, reply, click, follow, DM, or conversion.
- Proof, link, asset, or claim source.
- Disclosure needs for partner, affiliate, paid, employee, gifted, or sponsored content.
- Manual publish notes: media, tags, alt text, scheduling, moderation, and analytics owner.

## Output Shape

- 3 to 5 post variants when useful, each optimized for a different hook or angle.
- Optional visual brief: image/video subject, crop, overlay text, alt text, and rights notes.
- Optional reply prompts for likely comments or objections.
- Manual handoff notes for links, tags, scheduling, disclosures, and measurement.

Audience-facing copy must not include internal labels such as `hook:`, `CTA:`, or `note:`.

## Copy And Creative Rules

- Put the value, tension, or news in the first visible line.
- Use one idea per post. If there are multiple beats, use a thread, carousel, or video instead.
- Make the post concrete with a number, example, artifact, quote, before/after, screenshot, or specific observation.
- Use links intentionally. Some surfaces reward native discussion before off-platform clicks.
- Make the close proportional: a question for discussion, CTA for action, or no close when the statement should stand.
- Add alt text for meaningful images and describe visual requirements clearly.

## Psychological Levers

- Relevance: name the audience situation precisely.
- Social proof: use verifiable customer, community, or peer signals only when naturally true.
- Authority: show expertise through a useful observation, not status theater.
- Curiosity: open a loop only if the post pays it off quickly.
- Commitment: ask for a low-friction action that matches intent.

Do not use fake urgency, guilt, shame, vague fear, or bait questions to manufacture engagement.

## Platform Adaptation

- Instagram/Facebook: asset-first, caption supports visual, avoid hashtag stuffing.
- X: concise, timely, specific, and able to stand alone in reposts or replies.
- Threads/Bluesky/Mastodon: conversational, community-aware, less tolerant of brand automation.
- Pinterest: search-like visual discovery; write descriptive titles/captions and prioritize useful imagery.
- LinkedIn/Reddit/Blog/Website use dedicated adapters where routed by the registry.

## Variants And Testing

- Test one variable at a time: hook, proof type, asset, CTA, post length, or link placement.
- Judge by qualified replies, saves, shares, clicks, profile visits, follows, and downstream action.
- Keep a note of the segment, post angle, asset type, and timing so results teach future drafts.

## Review Checklist

- The post has one clear audience and one primary job.
- The first line or asset earns attention without misleading.
- Claims, links, tags, disclosures, and rights are verified or flagged.
- The post sounds native to the selected platform and account.
- Manual publishing, moderation, and measurement tasks are separate from final copy.

## Failure Diagnostics

- Low reach: check topic fit, account context, timing, asset quality, and platform-native formatting.
- Low engagement: check whether the post has a clear reason to reply/save/share.
- Low clicks/conversions: check CTA clarity, link promise, landing-page match, and audience intent.
- Negative replies: check claims, tone, missing context, community norms, and disclosure.

## Anti-Patterns

- Cross-posting the same exact copy everywhere.
- Asking "thoughts?" when no real discussion is invited.
- Using vague hype instead of concrete value.
- Relying on hashtags to make a weak post discoverable.
- Turning every post into a sales pitch.
