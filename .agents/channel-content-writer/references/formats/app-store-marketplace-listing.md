# App Store And Marketplace Listing Format Playbook

Use this format for app-store pages, marketplace listings, integration pages, extension listings, and product directories. Load `../channels/direct-product.md` and `../channels/app-store-marketplace.md` first.

## Source Guidance

- Apple App Store product page guidance: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/
- Google Play store-listing best practices: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13393723
- Google Play Developer Policy Center: https://play.google.com/about/developer-content-policy/
- FTC endorsement guidance for reviews/testimonials: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides

## Use Cases

- Help users install, trial, buy, or evaluate an app, plugin, extension, integration, template, or marketplace product.
- Improve search visibility and conversion while staying policy-compliant.

## Required Inputs

- Platform, category, product name, audience, main use case, keyword priorities, and competitor alternatives.
- Features, benefits, screenshots, preview video, proof, pricing, compatibility, privacy/security claims, and support links.
- Policy constraints, localization needs, and manual submission owner.

## Output Shape

- Title/name, subtitle/short description, long description, what's-new/release note, keyword notes where allowed, screenshot captions, preview-video concept, and FAQ/support notes.
- Policy and claim risks.
- Manual asset/submission/localization checklist.

## Copy And Creative Rules

- Lead with what the product does and the user outcome.
- Translate features into evaluation criteria: faster setup, fewer errors, easier reporting, better workflow, safer collaboration.
- Use keywords naturally and only where the platform supports them.
- Match copy to screenshots. The first visual assets should show the core product value.
- Include trust details when they matter: permissions, integrations, security, privacy, support, compatibility, plan requirements.
- Keep release notes user-facing: what changed, who benefits, and what to try.

## Psychological Levers

- Clarity reduces install risk.
- Visual proof helps users imagine successful use.
- Authority comes from verified reviews, certifications, integrations, or customer proof when policy-safe.
- Risk reduction: compatibility, privacy, pricing, and support details reduce hesitation.
- Specificity beats superlatives.

## Platform Adaptation

- Apple App Store: metadata and first screenshots/previews shape discovery and conversion.
- Google Play: short description, feature graphic, screenshots, and policy-compliant claims matter heavily.
- B2B marketplace: prioritize integration fit, permissions, admin setup, docs, support, and procurement confidence.
- Plugin/extension directory: emphasize compatibility, installation steps, and use cases.

## Variants And Testing

- Test screenshot order, short description, title/subtitle, icon, video, proof, and localization.
- Track impressions, page views, conversion, installs, trial activation, review themes, uninstall, marketplace leads, and support volume.
- Use product-page optimization tools where available; do not infer causality from small traffic.

## Review Checklist

- Platform and policy constraints are explicit.
- Copy accurately reflects product functionality and screenshots.
- Keywords are natural and not stuffed.
- Claims, reviews, customer names, privacy, compatibility, and pricing are verified or flagged.
- Manual upload, asset, localization, and approval steps are listed.

## Failure Diagnostics

- Low impressions: weak keyword/category fit or discoverability metadata.
- Low conversion: first screenshots weak, promise unclear, reviews poor, or trust info missing.
- Review rejection: inaccurate metadata, deceptive claims, restricted content, or asset policy issue.
- High uninstall: listing promise does not match product reality.

## Anti-Patterns

- "The best app for everyone" positioning.
- Screenshots that do not show the app in use.
- Keyword dumping.
- Unsupported security/privacy claims.
- Ignoring review objections in the listing.
