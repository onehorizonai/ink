# Forum And Q&A Post Format Playbook

Use this format for forums, Hacker News-style discussions, Quora answers, support-community answers, and searchable Q&A surfaces. Load `../channels/communities.md` first. Reddit routes to its dedicated skill.

## Source Guidance

- Hacker News Guidelines: https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html
- Quora Platform Policies and business context: https://help.quora.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000470706-Platform-Policies and https://business.quora.com/advertising/for-advertisers
- Discord/Slack community conduct references where the Q&A happens inside those spaces: https://discord.com/guidelines/ and https://api.slack.com/docs/community-code-of-conduct

## Use Cases

- Ask a clear question, answer a real question, share a technical explanation, respond to a product category discussion, or create a durable searchable answer.

## Required Inputs

- Forum/Q&A site, community rules, topic, audience expertise, affiliation, and whether posting as brand/person.
- Question wording or thread context.
- Evidence, sources, examples, product limitations, and what should not be promoted.

## Output Shape

- Title/question or answer body.
- Context, direct answer, explanation, examples, caveats, and optional link/resource.
- Disclosure line when affiliation matters.
- Suggested follow-up replies and manual moderation notes.

## Copy And Creative Rules

- Answer the question directly before adding context.
- Make the answer useful even if the reader never clicks a link.
- Use concrete examples, steps, tradeoffs, and caveats.
- Be honest about uncertainty and limitations.
- Put affiliation near the first relevant mention, not hidden at the end.
- For Hacker News-style contexts, keep tone substantive, curious, and non-promotional.
- For Quora-style answers, structure for skimming and search durability.

## Psychological Levers

- Helpfulness drives trust more than persuasion.
- Authority is earned through clear reasoning and evidence.
- Reciprocity comes from giving a complete answer.
- Reactance decreases when links are optional and affiliation is transparent.
- Specificity makes answers feel credible and memorable.

## Platform Adaptation

- Hacker News: avoid marketing tone, shallow takes, title bait, and self-promotion. Expect technical scrutiny.
- Quora: answer the exact question, use clear structure, disclose affiliations, and avoid unsupported authority.
- Product/support forums: prioritize reproducible steps, versions, expected behavior, and links to docs.
- Community Q&A: match local norms for length, tagging, and follow-up.

## Variants And Testing

- Test title clarity, depth, examples, source placement, and CTA/link presence.
- Track accepted answers, upvotes, helpful votes, replies, referral clicks, follow-up questions, and moderation outcomes.
- Review objections to improve docs and future answers.

## Review Checklist

- The answer is useful without clicking away.
- Affiliation and commercial interest are disclosed.
- Claims and sources are accurate or flagged.
- Tone fits the forum's norms.
- Follow-up responsibilities are clear.

## Failure Diagnostics

- Downvotes/negative replies: too promotional, shallow, wrong community, or unsupported claim.
- Low visibility: title unclear, question mismatch, bad timing, or weak search terms.
- No conversions: answer solved awareness but did not match purchase intent.
- Moderation removal: rules, disclosure, link policy, or duplicate-post issue.

## Anti-Patterns

- Posting a disguised ad as an answer.
- Link-only answers.
- Ignoring the actual question.
- Pretending certainty on unresolved topics.
- Arguing defensively with skeptical replies.
