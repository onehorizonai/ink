# Short Video Format Playbook

Use this format for TikTok videos, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, social clips, and other vertical short-form video handoffs. Load the relevant channel guide first.

## Source Guidance

- YouTube Shorts guidance and mobile filming tips: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13363900 and https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12948118
- TikTok Creative Center and Creative Codes: https://ads.us.tiktok.com/help/article/creative-center and https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-CA/creative-codes
- Meta Instagram creator best practices: https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/best-practices-education-hub-creators-instagram/
- X vertical video ad specs when used for paid social: https://business.x.com/en/help/campaign-setup/creative-ad-specifications

## Use Cases

- Show a quick tip, demo, before/after, mistake, reaction, proof point, product moment, founder POV, trend response, or clip from longer content.
- Make a single idea memorable in a mobile-first feed.
- Drive saves, shares, follows, comments, profile visits, clicks, or retargeting audiences.

## Required Inputs

- Platform, audience, topic, single payoff, and desired action.
- Talent/voice: founder, creator, customer, narrator, product screen, hands-only, or animation.
- Available assets: footage, screenshots, B-roll, music/sound rights, captions, brand constraints.
- Claims/proof and disclosures.
- Manual production owner and editing constraints.

## Output Shape

- Hook options for first second and first spoken/on-screen line.
- Beat-by-beat script with approximate timing.
- Shot list and editing notes.
- On-screen text, captions/subtitles, and safe-area notes.
- Caption, hashtags/keywords where relevant, cover idea, CTA, and manual QA notes.

## Copy And Creative Rules

- Make the topic legible instantly. The viewer should know what kind of video this is before deciding to swipe.
- Start with a visual event, concrete sentence, strong contrast, or problem recognition.
- Use one core idea. If the script needs multiple ideas, split into a series.
- Write spoken lines in natural speech, not ad copy.
- Change the frame, example, or information every few seconds to sustain attention.
- Use captions/subtitles and readable on-screen text. Sound should improve the video, not be required for comprehension unless platform strategy says otherwise.
- Show the product, process, person, or proof whenever possible.

## Psychological Levers

- Pattern interruption: a surprising first frame or line earns the pause.
- Completion: loops, reveals, and step counts sustain watching.
- Identification: name the viewer's exact situation.
- Ability: make the next action tiny and obvious.
- Social proof: show real use, not vague popularity.

Avoid fear hooks, fake mistakes, staged outrage, or misleading "wait until the end" structures.

## Platform Adaptation

- TikTok: conversational, creator-native, search-aware captions, trend-aware but not trend-dependent.
- Instagram Reels: original visual polish, strong cover, save/share value, clear safe areas.
- YouTube Shorts: first-second clarity, title/description alignment, channel context, and loop/payoff.
- Paid social: brand and product should appear early enough to be remembered; match specs and CTA behavior.

## Variants And Testing

- Test hook type, talent, length, first frame, caption, audio, CTA, and proof type.
- Track viewed-versus-swiped, retention, average watch time, completion, rewatches, saves, shares, comments, follows, and conversion.
- Do not judge a video only by views. Qualified comments and downstream action can matter more.

## Review Checklist

- The first second is clear and non-misleading.
- The script is speakable and concrete.
- Shot list, captions, cover, safe areas, and rights notes are included.
- Claims, customer examples, music, footage, and disclosures are verified or flagged.
- Manual recording, editing, publishing, and analytics tasks are explicit.

## Failure Diagnostics

- Fast swipe-away: hook is unclear, first frame weak, or topic too broad.
- Low completion: pacing drags, payoff delayed, or too many ideas.
- Low saves/shares: video lacks reusable insight or emotional recognition.
- Low conversion: CTA mismatched, product not shown, or landing path unclear.

## Anti-Patterns

- Logo intros before context.
- Dense talking-head explanations with no visual changes.
- Captions that cover important visuals.
- Trend use without audience fit.
- Clickbait hooks that the video does not satisfy.
