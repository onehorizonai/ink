# Paid Ad Format Playbook

Use this format for paid social, paid search, sponsored placements, retargeting creative, and ad-copy handoffs. Load `../channels/paid-partner.md` first and any platform channel guide where available.

## Source Guidance

- Google Ads search ad best practices: https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6167122
- Meta ad creative guidance: https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/ad-creative
- X ad creative specifications: https://business.x.com/en/help/campaign-setup/creative-ad-specifications
- Reddit ad creative best practices: https://www.business.reddit.com/copy-creative-best-practices
- Pinterest creative best practices and ad policy: https://business.pinterest.com/creative-best-practices/ and https://policy.pinterest.com/en/advertising-guidelines
- FTC endorsement and advertising disclosure guidance: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides

## Use Cases

- Generate search clicks, social demand, retarget visitors, promote events/resources, drive installs, test offers, or support partner/sponsored distribution.

## Required Inputs

- Platform, objective, audience/keywords, funnel stage, offer, landing page, budget/test constraints, and conversion event.
- Product proof, claims, creative assets, compliance constraints, and brand voice.
- Ad type: search, image, video, carousel, lead form, app install, retargeting, or sponsored post.

## Output Shape

- Campaign/ad set assumptions and audience intent.
- Ad variants with headline, primary text, description, CTA, display URL/path, and creative direction as platform requires.
- Landing-page match notes, claim/disclosure risks, and manual setup QA.

## Copy And Creative Rules

- Match intent. Search ads answer a query; social ads interrupt a feed; retargeting ads continue a known journey.
- One ad should communicate one promise or offer.
- Use concrete benefits, differentiators, proof, or urgency only when true.
- For search, include keyword-intent language naturally and create multiple assets for responsive ads.
- For social, make the visual/first frame do as much work as the headline.
- For retargeting, acknowledge known context carefully without sounding invasive.
- CTA should match funnel stage: learn, compare, download, book, install, start trial, buy, or return.

## Psychological Levers

- Relevance: query/audience fit is more persuasive than clever copy.
- Fluency: simple, specific ads reduce decision effort.
- Social proof: use real customer/market proof when verifiable.
- Scarcity/urgency: use only for real deadlines or availability.
- Risk reduction: free trial, demo, guarantee, proof, or transparent pricing can reduce hesitation.

## Platform Adaptation

- Paid search: query intent, responsive headlines/descriptions, business name/logo assets, landing-page relevance.
- Meta/Instagram: creative diversification, mobile-first visuals, platform placements, early product/brand signal.
- TikTok/Reels/Shorts ads: short-video rules, creator-native style, clear first seconds.
- Reddit: conversational, community-aware, mobile-first, clear branding without pretending to be a user.
- Pinterest: inspirational/search visual, high-quality product/context imagery, policy-safe claims.

## Variants And Testing

- Test offer, audience, hook, proof, creative format, CTA, landing page, and funnel stage.
- Track CTR, CVR, CPA, ROAS, lead quality, pipeline, creative fatigue, frequency, landing-page bounce, and comment sentiment.
- Do not compare variants without enough spend, consistent audiences, and clean conversion tracking.

## Review Checklist

- Platform, objective, audience, conversion event, and landing page are explicit.
- Ad promise matches the landing page.
- Claims, disclosures, targeting sensitivity, and policy risks are flagged.
- Creative requirements and manual ad-platform setup are clear.
- Variants test meaningful differences, not random wording.

## Failure Diagnostics

- Low CTR: weak intent match, vague headline, poor visual, or wrong audience.
- Low conversion: landing-page mismatch, weak offer, slow page, or wrong funnel stage.
- High CPA: poor targeting, creative fatigue, low trust, or conversion tracking issue.
- Policy rejection: prohibited claims, missing disclosures, sensitive attributes, or restricted category.

## Anti-Patterns

- Writing social ad copy like search ad copy.
- Using fake scarcity or unsupported superlatives.
- Testing too many variables at once.
- Sending all traffic to a generic homepage.
- Hiding sponsorship, affiliate, or material connections.
