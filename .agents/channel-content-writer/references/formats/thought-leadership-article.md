# Thought Leadership Article Format

## Source Guidance

Use `../channels/editorial.md` and the destination's own rules. For e27, load `../channels/e27.md`. The structure below is Ink's recommendation, not a publisher-mandated template.

## Use Cases

Contributor opinion, an operator's lesson, or evidence-led analysis for an external publication. Pitch-only requests should stop at a pitch.

## Required Inputs

- Destination, author, reader, thesis, and why the argument matters now.
- A real incident or primary evidence, publication history, and commercial relationships.
- The selected-author publication brief and destination rules.

If the argument depends on a missing personal incident, request the facts or use attributed external evidence. Never fill the gap with a fictional first-person story.

## Output Shape

Return only the requested stage:

- Pitch: working headline, thesis, reader payoff, evidence plan, and distinct contribution.
- Draft: headline, optional standfirst, article, and a separate source/claims ledger.
- Submission package: reviewed article, word count, short author bio, publication-history note, image/credit choice, and remaining manual steps.

Do not insert source ledgers, review labels, sales CTAs, or production notes into the article body.

## Copy And Creative Rules

Use one argument. A workable structure is a short opening problem, explanation of why it happens, a concrete example, a serious counterpoint, and a practical implication. Use connected paragraphs and descriptive subheadings. Keep technical detail only when it helps a founder make a decision.

## Psychological Levers

Earn trust through specificity, honest limits, and a useful decision. Curiosity should reveal an argument; it should not withhold the point until the end.

## Platform Adaptation

The destination guide owns length, link rules, regional fit, and submission constraints. An owned blog's SEO requirements, internal links, and product conversion goals do not transfer automatically.

## Variants And Testing

If the direction is unclear, propose at most three theses. Select for evidence, author authority, audience usefulness, and novelty against the publication's recent coverage. A new headline on the same argument is not a new contribution.

## Review Checklist

Run `content-humanizer`, `content-tone-review`, and `content-style-review` in order; use `fact-check` and `source-url-check` for external claims and links. Recheck the destination rules after revision. Report specific unresolved claims separately and do not label an unverified draft submission-ready.

## Failure Diagnostics

- Generic argument: narrow it to a decision and show the mechanism.
- Thin authority: get a real example or reduce the claim.
- Promotional drift: remove the company and test whether the argument still works.
- Repeated coverage: add a distinct operating detail or choose another thesis.

## Anti-Patterns

Feature tours, numbered tip collections without an argument, fake certainty, borrowed autobiographical detail, padded intros, and conclusions that turn into a product pitch.
