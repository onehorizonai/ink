# Community Post Format Playbook

Use this format for Discord, Slack communities, forums, Product Hunt comments, community announcements, member prompts, and non-dedicated community channels. Load `../channels/communities.md` and any specific channel guide first.

## Source Guidance

- Discord Community Guidelines and Community Server Guidelines: https://discord.com/guidelines/ and https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035969312-Community-Server-Guidelines
- Slack Community Code of Conduct: https://api.slack.com/docs/community-code-of-conduct
- Product Hunt Launch Guide: https://www.producthunt.com/launch/
- Reddit Business creative guidance for community-native tone, even when Reddit routes to a dedicated skill: https://www.business.reddit.com/copy-creative-best-practices

## Use Cases

- Announce a useful update, ask for feedback, invite participation, share a resource, start a discussion, answer a community pattern, or coordinate an event.
- Support communities where trust, moderation, and reciprocity matter more than reach.

## Required Inputs

- Community/channel, rules, audience relationship, moderation context, and permission to post.
- Post purpose, desired response, links/resources, and what value the community gets.
- Disclosure needs for employees, partners, affiliates, sponsors, or commercial asks.

## Output Shape

- Post copy tailored to the community surface.
- Optional title, channel recommendation, thread/reply structure, and moderation notes.
- Suggested replies for likely questions or objections.
- Manual posting, tagging, timing, and follow-up responsibilities.

## Copy And Creative Rules

- Lead with community value before brand value.
- Use the community's vocabulary, but do not fake insider status.
- Be transparent about role, affiliation, and ask.
- Make participation easy: one question, one poll, one link, one next step, or one requested type of feedback.
- For announcements, explain who is affected, what changed, why it matters, and where to ask questions.
- For feedback asks, specify the decision the feedback will inform.

## Psychological Levers

- Reciprocity: contribute useful context before requesting attention.
- Belonging: respect shared norms and member identity.
- Autonomy: invite, do not pressure.
- Commitment: ask for small participation such as one reply, vote, or example.
- Trust: be clear about affiliation and intent.

## Platform Adaptation

- Discord: channel fit, concise posts, moderation safety, event/thread/channel mechanics.
- Slack community: work-context clarity, respectful pings, thread discipline, and no noisy broadcasts.
- Product Hunt: maker transparency, feedback loops, and thoughtful public replies.
- Forum/community board: searchable title, context, tags, and long-term usefulness.

## Variants And Testing

- Test title, first sentence, question type, timing, format, and depth.
- Track replies, useful feedback, reactions, attendance, clicks, member sentiment, moderation load, and downstream outcomes.
- Do not optimize for reaction count if discussion quality or trust declines.

## Review Checklist

- Post follows the community's explicit rules and implicit norms.
- The value to members is clear before the ask.
- Affiliation, commercial intent, and disclosures are clear.
- Pings/tags are justified and minimal.
- Manual moderation and follow-up owner are named.

## Failure Diagnostics

- No replies: ask too broad, wrong channel, weak member value, or bad timing.
- Negative replies: affiliation hidden, self-promotion too high, or norm mismatch.
- Moderator issue: rules not checked or title/post looked like spam.
- Low conversion: community did not have enough intent for the ask.

## Anti-Patterns

- Drive-by promotion.
- Pretending to be a neutral member when posting for a brand.
- Tagging everyone for low-stakes updates.
- Asking vague "feedback?" with no context.
- Posting and disappearing.
