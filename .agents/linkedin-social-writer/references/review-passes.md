# Review Passes

## Order

For posts, use this default order:

1. humanizer
2. tone review
3. style review
4. fact check when needed
5. final self-edit for longer drafts

## Humanizer

Goal:

- remove AI tells early so later passes review real writing instead of machine noise

Look for:

- em dashes
- canned transitions
- buzzword filler
- mechanical symmetry
- generic hype
- overclean paragraph rhythm

## Tone review

Goal:

- compare the draft against the saved voice, stance, and intensity in the corpus

Look for:

- wrong sentence rhythm
- wrong CTA pressure
- wrong level of personal detail
- wrong mix of directness and reflection
- irrelevant local context details

## Style review

Goal:

- improve clarity, flow, structure, and LinkedIn readability

Look for:

- weak hook
- weak first visible line before `read more` on longer posts
- too many ideas in one post
- paragraph bloat
- fuzzy proof
- forced question prompts
- engagement levers that feel bolted on instead of helping the reader
- links, videos, or carousels that do not add enough value to justify the click or swipe
- soft close or overly aggressive close

## Fact check

Goal:

- verify unstable or external claims before finalizing

Use when the draft includes:

- dates
- numbers
- company or product facts
- sourced claims
- public events
- legal, financial, medical, or regulatory references

Use the local MCP research tools described in `references/mcp-tools.md` when available.

## Extra pass threshold

Add one more pass after the normal loop when:

- the post is longer than roughly 180 words
- the post mixes story and claims
- the post uses a strong opinion that could overstate facts
- the user says the post is important or high-stakes
