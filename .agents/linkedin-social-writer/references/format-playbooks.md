# Format Playbooks

## Post

Purpose:

- publish a standalone idea, story, lesson, opinion, launch, or invitation

Build with:

- a hook that sounds like the archive
- a valuable takeaway or story, not filler posted just to stay active
- a clear core idea
- proof, example, or explanation
- short paragraphs and bullets when they help the post scan fast
- authority-building detail drawn from lived experience, results, or sharp observation
- a close that fits the usual CTA intensity and ends with a small set of relevant hashtags

Optional optimization levers:

- on longer posts, make the first visible sentence strong enough to earn the `read more` click
- use a question when the goal is genuine conversation and the post gives people something real to answer
- use a link, video, or carousel when it deepens the point or gives the reader a useful next action
- skip these levers when they make the post feel engineered instead of useful

Check:

- the first lines earn the reader's attention without sounding borrowed
- if the post is long, the first visible sentence makes the hidden rest feel worth opening
- the body holds one central point
- the story or example creates emotional connection without drifting into fluff
- the structure is easy to scan on mobile
- the post builds credibility through specific experience, proof, or judgment
- if a question is used, it invites a real response instead of baiting for comments
- if a link, video, or carousel is used, it adds real value instead of interrupting the post
- any engagement lever supports the point instead of hijacking it
- the hashtags are relevant and restrained
- the close does not become salesier than the archive

Avoid:

- empty “anyone else?” engagement bait
- bolted-on questions that do not fit the point of the post
- curiosity-gap openings that overpromise what the post will deliver
- vague inspiration without substance
- dense text blocks that slow down scanning
- generic claims of authority without proof
- links or assets that distract from the main takeaway
- hashtag spam or irrelevant tags

## Comment Reply

Purpose:

- continue a thread, answer a point, thank someone, clarify a claim, or deepen the conversation

Build with:

- direct acknowledgment of the actual comment
- one concise response or extension
- optional follow-up question only if it feels natural

Check:

- the reply makes sense if read next to the original comment
- the tone matches the relationship and thread energy
- the reply does not pivot into a pitch

Avoid:

- repeating the original comment back at length
- turning every reply into a CTA
- sounding scripted

## DM

Purpose:

- start a 1:1 conversation, share something relevant, or earn a next step

Build with:

- a specific reason for reaching out
- one clear idea or offer
- a low-friction close

Check:

- the opener proves this is not mass outreach
- the value is concrete
- the ask is proportionate to the relationship

Avoid:

- long paragraphs
- multiple asks in one message
- generic compliments that could fit anyone

## DM Reply

Purpose:

- answer the inbound message directly and move the conversation forward

Build with:

- a direct response to the inbound note
- the most relevant context or clarification
- one next action, question, or confirmation

Check:

- the reply resolves the main point before adding anything new
- the cadence sounds human
- the thread moves forward without pressure

Avoid:

- ignoring the sender's actual question
- overexplaining
- adding friction with too many decisions

## Repost

Purpose:

- share someone else's post while adding the author's own angle

Build with:

- a clear reason this matters
- one interpretation, lesson, or disagreement
- optional bridge back to the author's audience or work

Check:

- the commentary adds value beyond praise
- the focus stays on the new thought, not on generic approval
- the post still sounds like the archive

Avoid:

- “Great post” wrappers
- repeating the original post without interpretation
- forcing a CTA when the archive would normally stay reflective
