# Repetition Guard

Use this when drafting or reviewing LinkedIn, Reddit, and blog copy. The goal is freshness within the author's voice, not novelty for its own sake.

## Compare Against

- the current draft
- 3-7 relevant corpus examples for the same channel
- recent drafts or published posts in the same topic area when available
- subreddit or format examples only for pattern study, not phrasing reuse

## Check For

- repeated opening moves, especially the same question, contrast, confession, or "most people miss" setup
- repeated close moves, especially the same soft CTA, "what do you think" prompt, or lesson summary
- reused sentence shapes across posts, even when the words are different
- recurring transition phrases that make separate posts feel generated from one template
- repeated metaphors, punchlines, and binary contrasts
- pleonasm and redundant phrasing, where nearby words repeat the same meaning without adding force or precision
- topic-level repetition where the same claim is restated without a new example, proof point, or sharper angle
- corpus mimicry: a draft that sounds like it was remixed from one saved post instead of written fresh

## Fix By

- keeping the author's real cadence but changing the rhetorical move
- replacing familiar abstractions with a concrete example, constraint, number, or scene
- varying sentence length and paragraph shape where the corpus supports that range
- changing the opening job: scene, claim, tension, observation, or consequence
- changing the close job: implication, decision rule, question, caveat, or next action
- cutting repeated claims instead of paraphrasing them
- cutting redundant modifiers, doubled verbs, and repeated qualifiers unless the repetition creates intentional rhythm or emphasis

## Do Not Do

- do not add novelty that weakens the argument or breaks channel fit
- do not avoid useful signature phrasing when it is rare, intentional, and fits the brief
- do not copy a corpus sentence because it sounds authentic
- do not solve repetition by making the draft more generic

## Output Note

When a repetition check changes the draft, report the reused pattern you removed in one short sentence.
