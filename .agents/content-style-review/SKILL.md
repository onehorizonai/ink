# Content Style Review

Focus on structure and readability, not on identity or factual verification. Follow the active writer workflow for the exact place of this pass in the sequence.

## Read

- the active writer skill's workflow file
- the active writer skill's review-passes file
- the active writer skill's format-playbooks file if one exists
- `../social-common/references/repetition-guard.md`

## Use

- the copy clarity and specificity principles from `../copywriting/SKILL.md`
- the format rules in the active writer playbooks

## Check

- hook strength
- one clear core idea or throughline
- section and paragraph flow
- prose-first flow instead of list-shaped scaffolding
- proof and specificity
- close quality
- unnecessary repetition
- repeated openings, closes, transitions, sentence shapes, and claims across the current draft and relevant corpus examples
- for blog articles, `---` between major sections when clearer pacing needs it

## Guardrails

- do not rewrite into a landing page
- do not make every close promotional
- do not turn prose into bullets just because it looks tidier
- for blog articles, do not polish away the sharper editorial edge into generic SaaS content
- do not override the corpus voice for the sake of polish
- do not fix repetition by sanding the draft into generic copy

## Output

Return:

- the tightened draft
- the main structure or clarity changes
- any repeated wording or sentence pattern you removed
