# Content Idea Brief Template

Use this as the `description` for the One Horizon idea record. Blog ideas follow the Blog Lifecycle Contract.
Generic non-specialized channel ideas may live under `Ink - Channel Content`.

This brief is the handoff artifact. It should be complete enough for a later agent to pick up after One Horizon approval without rereading the whole ideation thread.

```md
## Decision
Status: Proposed
Decision location: One Horizon
Do not draft yet: this brief is waiting for approval.

## Idea
Working title: <title>
Recommended channel: <canonical channel slug or dedicated channel name>
Angle: <one-sentence angle>
Audience: <specific reader/community>
Goal: <awareness | education | demand capture | trust | recruiting | launch support | other>

## Why This
Why now: <timely signal or evergreen reason>
Company fit: <why this is credible for the author/company>
Archive gap: <what LinkedIn, Reddit, and blog archive checks showed>

## Sources
- <url or source note>

## Pick-Up Brief
Thesis: <what the content should argue or explore>
Proof needed: <examples, screenshots, customer evidence, data, or claims to verify>
Risks: <promotion risk, weak proof, sensitive claim, subreddit-fit risk, SEO mismatch, etc.>
Next workflow: <linkedin-social-writer | reddit-research then reddit-social-writer | blog-post-writer | page-brief-builder | channel-content-writer | content-program-runner | manual-external-tool>

## Channel Notes
Blog type: <only for Blog; treat as confirmed once the idea is approved and moved to Planned unless the reviewer changes it>
Reddit research need: <only for Reddit; include likely subreddit/audience assumptions for reddit-research to verify>
Generic channel notes: <only for unsupported channels; include channel-native constraints, asset needs, and manual execution steps>

## Program Metadata
Program id: <optional; omit when this is a one-off idea>
Format id: <optional>
Run id: <optional>
Campaign id: <optional>
```

Rules:

- Preserve the headings.
- Omit only irrelevant channel-specific fields from `Channel Notes`.
- Omit `Program Metadata` when the idea does not belong to a Content Program.
- Do not include drafted content.
- Do not ask for approval in chat. Approval happens in One Horizon.
