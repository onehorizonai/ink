# Channel Fit

## Choose LinkedIn when

- the idea reacts to something timely or visible now
- the point can land in one sharp claim, lesson, or founder observation
- it benefits from personality, narrative, or a product-in-the-wild screenshot
- the goal is conversation, launch awareness, recruiting, or founder signal

Good fits:

- a contrarian take on a rising discussion
- a product ship note tied to a workflow pain
- a conference or event reaction with a clear lesson
- a short proof-led opinion on AI tooling adoption

## Choose The Blog when

- the reader needs explanation, comparison, or step-by-step reasoning
- the angle can support search intent or durable reference content
- the claim needs more proof, examples, or screenshots than a short post can carry
- the goal is education, demand capture, durable positioning, or sales-enablement content

Good fits:

- a comparison between roadmap-first and ticket-first AI workflows
- an explainer on spec-driven development with AI agents
- a deep dive on trust-first work capture versus surveillance tooling
- an operational guide on connecting tasks, PRs, and recaps

## Use Both when

- there is a strong evergreen blog thesis and a timely lighter LinkedIn entry point

Then:

- lead with LinkedIn when speed matters and the blog can follow
- lead with the blog when the idea needs proof first and LinkedIn can excerpt it later

## Avoid

- writing a blog post for a point that is only one paragraph deep
- stretching a nuanced framework into a short LinkedIn post with no room for proof
- repeating the same angle in both channels without changing the job
